Star Exams Privacy Policy

Effective 17 October 2026 · Applies to the Star Exams app for Android and Windows

In short:

  • We collect what we need to run your account and your practice: your name, phone number, school, region and study progress.
  • We do not show ads, do not use an advertising ID, do not use your GPS location and do not sell your information.
  • Your name, picture and scores are shown to other students only on contest and game leaderboards.
  • You can ask us to delete your account and its data at any time (see Deleting your account).

1. Who we are

Star Exams is an app that helps students in Ethiopia practise for the national exams. It is operated by Star Education Ethiopia ("we", "us"). Contact details are at the end of this policy.

2. Information we collect

Information you give us

  • Account: full name, phone number, password, school name, city, region and area, exam stream (natural or social), and the language you prefer for video lessons.
  • Profile: the avatar you choose.
  • Phone verification: if you verify your number through our Telegram bot, the phone number Telegram shares with us and your Telegram user ID.
  • AI study chat: the questions and messages you write, and the exam question or note you ask about.
  • Reports: mistakes you report in questions or notes, and what you write in them.
  • Payments (where they apply): the payment receipt picture you upload and your payment status. We do not receive your bank or card details.
  • Contests and games: the answers you submit and your results.

Information collected automatically

  • Device: a device identifier and device name, used so one account is used on one phone and one PC; the app version and whether you use Android or Windows; the days you opened the app.
  • Study progress: which questions you practised and how you did. Most of this stays on your device; contest and game results are stored with us.
  • Usage analytics: which screens you open, practice results and which features you use, through Google Analytics. This is linked to an account ID, never to your name or phone number.
  • Crash reports: technical details when the app crashes, so we can fix it.
  • Notifications: a notification token so we can send contest reminders and news, if you allow notifications.
  • Invitations: if you join through a friend's invite, which account invited you.
  • Post views: which posts in the Posts feed you have seen, so we know how many students a post reaches.

What we do not collect

We do not collect your precise (GPS) location, your contacts, photos other than ones you choose to upload, or an advertising ID. The app contains no advertising.

3. How we use it

  • To create your account, sign you in, and keep it secure (including the one-phone and one-PC rule).
  • To give you exams, notes, study packs, contests, games and the AI study chat.
  • To show contest and game leaderboards, and student counts and rankings by area and school (as totals, not individual details).
  • To award and track credits, invitations and rewards.
  • To send reminders and news you allowed in notifications, or through our Telegram channels if you join them.
  • To fix errors, understand which features help students, and improve the app.
  • To reply to your reports and support requests.

4. Who can see it

  • Other students see your name, avatar and score on contest and game leaderboards, and in games you join. They never see your phone number.
  • Trusted service providers that run parts of the app for us, such as hosting and storage, sign-in, notifications, analytics, file delivery and the AI study chat. They may use the information only to provide those services to us.
  • The law: we may disclose information if a valid legal request requires it, or to protect students' safety.

We do not sell or rent your information to anyone.

5. Where it is stored

Your information may be stored and processed on secure servers outside Ethiopia.

6. How long we keep it

  • Account, progress and chat history: while your account exists.
  • Analytics: under Google Analytics' retention settings, at most 14 months.
  • When you delete your account, we delete your personal information within 30 days, except what we must keep by law. Contest results may remain in totals, without your name.

7. Your choices and deleting your account

  • You can change your name, school, region and learning language in the app's Profile.
  • You can turn notifications off in your device settings.
  • To delete your account and its data, send a request from the phone number on your account to shemsedinremedan@gmail.com or our Telegram @Star_Exams_Helper, saying "Delete my Star Exams account". We confirm it is your account, then delete it within 30 days.
  • You can also ask us for a copy of your information, or to correct it, at the same contacts.

8. Children and young students

Star Exams is made for secondary-school students preparing for national exams, usually aged 14 and over. It is not directed at children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has given us information, contact us and we will delete it. If you are under 18, please use the app with your parent's or guardian's knowledge.

9. Security

Access to students' data is restricted by our server rules to the student and to us, and data travels over encrypted connections. No system is perfectly secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security, but we work to protect your information and will tell affected students if a breach puts them at risk.

10. Changes to this policy

When we change this policy we update the date at the top. If a change matters, we also tell you in the app or our Telegram channel.
