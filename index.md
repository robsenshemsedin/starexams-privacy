# StarExams – Account and Data Deletion

This page explains how users of **StarExams** can request deletion of their account and personal data.

## How to request account deletion

If you want to delete your StarExams account, please send an email to:

**shemsedinremedan@gmail.com**

In your email, please include:

- Your full name (as used in the app)  
- Your phone number (used in registration)  
- A clear request, for example: “I want to delete my StarExams account and all my personal data.”

We will process your request as soon as possible.

## What data will be deleted

When you request account deletion, the following data will be deleted from our system:

- Your name  
- Your phone number  
- Your account credentials (username and password)  

After deletion, you will no longer be able to log in to StarExams with this account.

## Data retention

We do not keep your personal data after account deletion, except if we are required to keep some information for legal, security, or fraud-prevention reasons.  
In normal cases, all your account data (name, phone number, and password) is removed.
